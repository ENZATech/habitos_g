I built HabitOS because I was tired of habit apps locking basic features behind $5/month subscriptions, showing full-screen ads, and requiring a second to-do list app just to get through the day. I built HabitOS as a clean, native Android operating system for daily consistency. It's completely offline and private, gives you a full 7-day matrix view with streak counters so you can log your whole day in seconds, and even includes a built-in birthday tracker and wish generator to keep your personal connections strong.
